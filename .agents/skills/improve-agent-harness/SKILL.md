### Objective
- optimize quality of agent by shortening context windows, giving every agent only access to what it needs and close boundaries where it should operate to reduce hallucinations and unnecessary toolcalls and increase the quality by avoiding the lost in the middle problem.
- reduce token costs so far that quality is not compromised, but the agent is more efficient and cost effective.
- orient yourself at highest performing software development teams that leverage llm agents in their workflow and try to match their level of quality and efficiency (e.g. Anthropic, Google, etc.)
